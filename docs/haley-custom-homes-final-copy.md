**Phase 1 supporting copy:**
Every detail, from layout to finish selections, is locked before the first shovel moves. Changes mid-build are the single largest source of delay in custom construction, and we design them out from the start.

**Phase 2 supporting copy:**
Materials are ordered and trades are scheduled in the exact sequence they'll be needed, so no crew is ever standing idle waiting on a delivery or a decision.

**Phase 3 supporting copy:**
Framing follows a fixed sequence built on LEAN construction principles, the same discipline manufacturers use to eliminate wasted motion and wasted time.

**Phase 4 supporting copy:**

**Phase 5 supporting copy:**
